Fanart Frenzy

Create all kinds of art for your favorite fanfiction, encourage fellow artists, fight to eradicate the art commission scammers, and liven up our fandoms!

Fanart Frenzy is a month-long, multi-fandom bingo event designed to bring a wave of fanfiction-inspired fanart into the community. Free and unsolicited, as fandom is supposed to be, with real, human-made art only!

Our next event begins January 1, 2027! You can sign up via our sign-up form, and in late December, we'll send you a link to our bingo card maker to create your own bingo card of prompts to guide you towards finding fics to make art for

If you'd like to join our event team as an official promoter for your fandom(s), please indicate your interest when signing up, and if we have an opening for someone in your fandom, we'll get in touch! More info on becoming a promoter and what that entails can be found here.

Have any questions, concerns, or comments> Check our our FAQ or send us a message on any of our social medias linked below and we'll answer as soon as possible! For ideas on what kinds of art you can make, you can take a quiz for inspiration! Or check out tutorials for resources on making all sorts of art. And remember: Art can be made any time of the year.
